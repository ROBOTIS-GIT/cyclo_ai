# Power and battery — operator guidance only

Source: [Operation Guide](https://docs.robotis.com/docs/systems/aisapiens/quick_start_guide/operation_guide/), reviewed 2026-10-23. Fetch its current images and revision before onsite work; the agent cannot certify the physical setup.

Explain the documented sequence: with the robot off, align the battery as pictured, handles outward, and slide it straight until flush. Do not twist it. Confirm the surrounding area/support and physical E-STOP condition before the normal power-on sequence. The pack-side power button uses a one-second hold for on/off; confirm actual response. Remove the battery only after power-off, pulling straight by the handles. Controller power uses a hold until vibration/logo for on, or until the display turns off for off.

The latching red physical emergency stop cuts actuator power. Release/reset only after an onsite safety check and then follow normal startup/mode procedures. It is distinct from RC Damping. Do not automate release/reset or treat powered-off, Damping and E-STOP as synonyms.

Success indications come from observed hardware/controller state, not elapsed time alone. If power/battery fit differs from the manual, stop and obtain the matching revision. LED meanings and this page's mode-switch section remain Coming Soon; use the separate [RC reference](rc.md) for documented mode selection and do not invent LED codes.
