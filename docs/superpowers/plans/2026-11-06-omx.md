## Review focus

Native LeRobot must not inherit mandatory ROS/Zenoh setup. USB role is physical identity, not enumeration order. Preserve factory settings. Keep incompatible ROS operating modes and serial owners exclusive. Missing OMX configs must not select OMY/FFW. Uploaded datasets and long training jobs require concrete scope. Use only source-backed commands for the actual software revision.
